About

McGill Athletes in Business brings together varsity athletes from across McGill's teams and faculties who want a career in business.

The club was founded in 2025 and is run by students. New associates join through an application each fall.

Our members spend their summers in different cities, countries and industries, and bring what they learn back to the rest of the club.

  • Teams

    Men's soccer, men's basketball, women's basketball and more.

  • Studying

    Finance, economics, engineering and more.

  • Summer 2026

    Internships in London, New York, Toronto and Montreal.
